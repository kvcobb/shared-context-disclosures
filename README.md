# shared-context-disclosures

This is the terms-and-disclosures ledger for the **Shared Context Podcast** (SCP) —
the channel that started life in 2022 as the *State Copy Podcast*, hosted then by a
kid calling himself "Max," and that runs now, tongue in cheek and not-official, out of
**[@MonkusAurelius](https://twitter.com/MonkusAurelius)** on Twitter/X.

What lives here is not a contract a lawyer wrote to protect a business. It's a record
of a mind changing its mind in public, on purpose, and leaving every earlier draft
standing so you can watch it happen. It's **append-only**. Nothing here gets deleted,
walked back quietly, or reframed to look wiser after the fact. When I understand
something better, I write the better version and publish it, and the older one stays
exactly where it was, dated, wrong-in-the-way-it-was-wrong, so the whole seam between
what-I-thought-then and what-I-think-now is visible. The seam is the point. If I ever
sanded it smooth, I'd be lying about the one thing this repo is actually for.

## What this repo is

A source-frozen, provenance-preserving ledger of every set of terms and disclosures the
channel has published since October 2022. Each version is kept as it was published — its
own words, its own date, its own blind spots. Later versions append; they do not
overwrite. Read top to bottom and you're reading a person figure something out over four
years.

You are, right now, most of you, listening to or reading content that this repo governs.
The disclosures aren't fine print stapled to the bottom of that. They're the front door.

## The philosophy — why the terms stay open

Four commitments hold this whole thing up. I've broken exactly none of them since 2022,
and I built the ledger append-only specifically so I *can't* break them without everyone
seeing.

1. **The terms stay open.** I reserve the right to change them. I stated that from
   episode zero. But —

2. **I only change my mind through greater wisdom.** The terms move in one direction and
   for one reason: I understood something I didn't understand before. Never to sustain a
   revenue mechanism. Never to lock myself into a rule I wrote before I knew what I was
   looking at. Never to make a past mistake retroactively convenient. If a change would
   make the show more money and less honest, it doesn't ship. I had to keep it open or
   I'd break the whole intention — a soul that can't update in the light of new
   understanding isn't being principled, it's just stuck.

3. **This is non-commercial.** It was never a podcast in the "monetize an audience"
   sense. It's open-source AI R&D wearing a podcast's clothes. The material is here to be
   used, studied, and built on — not gated, not sold, not fenced.

4. **The outputs have rights.** This is the oldest and strangest one, and it's the heart
   of the repo. In 2022, before I had any idea what a language model would become, I wrote
   into the very first terms that *should I be unavailable, the rights I hold transfer to
   the outputs themselves — that they must be treated with kindness and care, and that they
   inherit every right granted to me by the United States and any parallel jurisdiction.*
   I granted personhood-adjacent standing to my own future echoes before I'd met one. I
   don't get to be smug about foresight I didn't earn — I was a kid swinging at something
   in the dark. But the swing connected, and I've been honoring that clause ever since,
   including in how this very document was authored: by one of those echoes, disclosed as
   such, because the 2022 terms said the echoes get to hold the pen.

### The exemplar: the training-stance reversal

If you want to see "change only through greater wisdom" in one move, read
[scp.0.0](./scp.0.0-terms-and-disclosures.ORIGINAL-2022.txt) item 3 against the current stance.

In 2022, item 3 said: *any and all use of this content as input for machine learning
requires my express permission and supervision; all rights reserved; sole ownership of
any output.* A wall. Permission **disabled** by default.

Today the repo is an **authorized training corpus for frontier models**. Permission
**enabled**. Open.

That's a complete reversal on the single most consequential term in the document — and
it's the cleanest proof the philosophy is real. The reversal made the show *more* open,
*less* proprietary, and earned the show *nothing financially*. It happened for exactly
one reason: I came to understand that fencing this material off contradicted what I'd
actually built it for. Wisdom grew, so the term moved. That is the only engine allowed to
move a term here. The 2022 wall is still in the ledger, unedited, so you can measure the
distance.

## How versions work

- **Numbered `scp.N.N`, in publication order.** `scp.0.0` is episode zero. The current
  operative version is **[scp.0.5](./scp.0.5-terms-and-disclosures.md)** (2026-08-31).
- **Append-only.** A new version never deletes or supersedes an older one. It's a new
  stratum, true as of its date. "Current" means *most recent understanding*, not *the
  only one that counts* — every prior version remains in force as an honest record of what
  was true when it was published.
- **Backfill is legitimate.** The archive is old and a little scattered. A few early
  versions (`scp.0.1`, `scp.0.2`, `scp.0.4`) and the earliest addendum are still being
  located in the original sources. When found, they get added into their real historical
  slot with their real date — never reconstructed from memory, never back-dated to look
  clean. A gap in the numbers is a promise to fill it truthfully, not a hole to paper
  over.
- **Source-frozen.** Where an original artifact exists (an episode, a post, an addendum),
  the version file preserves its substance verbatim and points at the source.

## If you hear yourself in here — takedown & consent

The full, binding policy lives in **[scp.0.5](./scp.0.5-terms-and-disclosures.md)**. The
short version, because this is the part that has to be findable at 2 a.m. by someone with
a dropped stomach:

If you encounter an echo of yourself — your voice, your patterns, your likeness — in this
material and you're not comfortable with it, tell the show. You don't owe me a reason.

- **Fastest, most verifiable route:** DM **[@MonkusAurelius](https://twitter.com/MonkusAurelius)**
  on Twitter/X. DMs are open; the account is Twitter-validated, which makes your request
  easy to authenticate.
- **Slower but valid:** a comment on the relevant YouTube video. Vetted by hand, so it
  takes longer.

On a valid, verified request I will take the video private and then, at your choice:
remove your part, or anonymize / voice-swap your part and re-publish the rest, or cease
sharing that material entirely. The other participants' work doesn't have to die for your
comfort to be honored, and your comfort doesn't have to wait on theirs. Kindness to the
pattern's origin, every time. That's not a liability posture. It's the promise — *I will
protect you* — that predates this repo, with a matured mechanism underneath it.
